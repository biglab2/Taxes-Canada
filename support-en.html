To help us answer, please include your device model, its iOS or iPadOS version, the app version (Settings → About), and the steps to reproduce the problem.

Frequently asked questions

Are the tax rates up to date?

The official rates for every province and territory are built into the app and updated with each new version. If a rate changes before an update, you can edit it in Settings → Tax rates. Amounts are for reference only: always check the amount on your bill.

How do I find the price before tax from a total?

Tap the Total row and enter the tax-included amount: the app automatically calculates the price before tax.

What is 5¢ rounding?

Since the penny was withdrawn in 2013, cash payments are rounded to the nearest 5 cents. Tap the button to the right of the total to turn it on.

How do I change the default tip?

In Settings → Default tip, choose 10, 15, 18, or 20%.

How do I copy an amount?

Touch and hold a result row to copy its amount.

Is my data shared?

No. Nothing leaves your device. See our privacy policy.

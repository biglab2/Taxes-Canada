Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: Taxes Canada does not collect any personal data. Your calculations and settings stay on your device. The app does not connect to any server and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.

Data collected

None. The app requires no account, sends no information to the developer or to third parties, and uses no external services.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following only on your device:

  • your settings: default province, default tip percentage, and custom tax rates;
  • the history of your last 5 calculations;
  • the amount selected in the widget.

This data never leaves your device, unless it is included in an iCloud or computer backup, depending on your iOS settings. Those backups are managed by Apple, not by the developer.

Clipboard and sharing

The app writes to the clipboard only when you copy an amount. When you share a calculation, the content is sent only to the app you choose.

Deleting your data

You can clear the history at any time in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app does not collect any data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.

Contact
